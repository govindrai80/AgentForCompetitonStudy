Published on the Insomniacs case studies page as "Selling Branded Land".

The transferable argument for a new prospect is the regional split: rather than
running one national creative, the campaign carried different propositions into
different metros — investment and USP-led in the developer's home markets,
escape-and-tranquility-led in the metros buying a second home at a distance.
That is the pattern worth reusing for any developer selling one asset into
several buyer geographies at once.
